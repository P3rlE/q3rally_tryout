Q3Rally Bountyhunter - asset sources
====================================

body.md3, wheel.md3, sheriff_main.tga, parts.tga, wheel.tga
    Based on PoliceCar.3DS, a rusty sheriff muscle car with its two
    hand-painted textures (sferiff.jpg, sferiffi.jpg), provided by the
    Q3Rally project (own model, same license as the game).

    Changes for Q3Rally (tools/carbuild/bountyhunter):
    - the original low-poly geometry (1,074 body and 140 wheel triangles)
      and textures, scaled to the size of the other cars, centred on the
      origin, tyres on the ground, normals with a crease angle
    - tags for wheels, lights, plate, exhaust, roof and the blower; the lamps
      and the plate are painted into the texture, their tags are placed where
      the painted parts sit on the model
    - the wheel keeps its own painted rim and tyre (tag_polygonwheel)

highway_main.tga, redline_main.tga, desert_main.tga, ghost_main.tga
    Recoloured from sferiff.jpg for Q3Rally (gen_textures.py): only the
    unsaturated paint changes colour, rust and details stay.

icon_*.tga
    Made for Q3Rally (render3d.py).

sound/player/bountyhunter/horn.ogg, falling1.ogg
    Shared with the Sidepipe.
