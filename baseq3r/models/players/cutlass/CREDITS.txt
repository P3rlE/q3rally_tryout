Cutlass (Q3Rally car)

Based on "Oldsmobile Cutlass Supreme Sedan '71" (https://skfb.ly/6QVZt)
by Barbo, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes for Q3Rally:
- converted from FBX to MD3, scaled and re-oriented, mesh decimated
  (paint, trim and chrome; grille and thin mouldings protected) from
  ~65k to ~12.5k triangles; a black panel closes the grille
- removed badges and script lettering, licence plate, tyres, interior
  and the clear light lenses
- new paint texture atlas with the original ambient occlusion baked in;
  new liveries (plain colours and Q3Rally rally schemes), flat trim,
  chrome and glass textures; the light texture is the original one
- tags for wheels, lights, weapon, plate, turbo and smoke added; the
  shared Q3Rally wheel replaces the original wheels

The scripts that did the conversion are in tools/carbuild
(build_olds.py, gen_olds_textures.py).
