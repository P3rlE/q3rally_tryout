Lightbody (Q3Rally car)

Based on "Lightbody Lifted '85 - Low poly model" (https://skfb.ly/pAMwJ)
by Daniel Zhabotinsky, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes for Q3Rally:
- converted from FBX to MD3, scaled and re-oriented; frame, suspension
  and roll bar decimated (body panels, bumpers, flares, bull bar and
  load bed kept as they are) from ~37k to ~16.5k triangles, UVs of the
  decimated parts transferred from the original mesh
- the empty grille opening is closed by a new honeycomb grille panel
- removed the radiator bits in the grille opening, the interior, steering wheel, badges, licence plate and the
  original tyres
- new paint texture atlas with new liveries (plain colours, an 80s
  two-tone and Q3Rally rally schemes); frame/lift kit and lamps use the
  original textures (downscaled), new glass texture
- tags for wheels, lights (including the roll bar lamps), weapon, plate,
  turbo and smoke added; the shared Q3Rally wheel replaces the original
  wheels

The scripts that did the conversion are in tools/carbuild
(build_lightbody.py, gen_lightbody_textures.py).
