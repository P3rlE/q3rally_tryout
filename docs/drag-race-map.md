# Drag Race maps

Three strips for the Drag Race mode, all generated by
`tools/maptools/make_dragstrip.py`:

| Map | Title | Distance | Setting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `q3r_dragstrip` | Desert Dragway | 1/4 mile | dragway in the desert by day: grandstands, paddock, timing boards, buttes, a blimp |
| `q3r_drag_night` | Night Drag | 1/8 mile | closed-off city avenue at night: building rows, neon, street lamps, spectators, an overpass |
| `q3r_drag_airfield` | Airfield Drag | 1/4 mile | runway of an airfield at sunset: hangars, tower, taxiway, bleachers, windsock |

Each `.arena` advertises only `q3r_drag`. `q3r_dragstrip` keeps the name of
the former test course, so Ladder results and menus stay valid.

The strip itself is identical on every map (the generator builds it with one
function): the same lanes, gates, start gantry with the two start lights,
walls, markings and the same asphalt surface (`SURF_ASPHALT`, Airfield uses
`textures/q3r_drag/runway`, a concrete look with the same surface flag).
White and yellow markings and the rubber of the burnout box and launch marks
are nonsolid, so they add no bumps and do not change tyre friction. There are
no weapons, pickups, bot routes or AAS files.

## Geometry contract

All driving is along positive X, at road height Z=0. The two numbered
`info_player_start` entities are `(0, -160, 28)` and `(0, 160, 28)`, facing
angle 0. The start positions have identical X and Z coordinates.

| Entity | Centre X | Y bounds | Z bounds |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `rally_start` | 100 | -320 to 320 | -8 to 192 |
| `rally_checkpoint`, number 1 | 7174 (1/8 mile: 3587) | -320 to 320 | -8 to 192 |
| `rally_finish` | 14348 (1/8 mile: 7174) | -320 to 320 | -8 to 192 |

The gate brushes are eight units thick. Their bounds, rather than an explicit
origin key, define their centres. The intermediate checkpoint is silent and
keeps the existing Sprint route and countdown infrastructure usable.

Drag timing measures the finish plane from each car's starting reference
position at X=0. With the default `CP_M_2_QU = (350 / 32.2) / 0.3048`,
the 14348-unit run is approximately 402.341 metres. This is a quarter mile
rounded to the map grid; 7174 units, the finish of Night Drag, are an eighth
mile (approximately 201.17 metres). The Sprint route's gate-to-gate length starts at
X=100, so it is approximately 2.804 metres shorter than that timed distance.

Lane centres are Y=-160 and Y=160. The middle marking has no physical barrier.
Roadside barriers start at Y=-384 and Y=384; both lanes have equal room.
On the quarter-mile maps the straight extends past the finish to the end wall
at X=19072 (a 96-unit crash barrier stands in front of it, X=18976), leaving
approximately 129.8 metres to brake. On Night Drag the end wall is at
X=10624 (crash barrier from X=10528), approximately 94.1 metres after the
eighth-mile finish. Start and finish
gantries clear the road by 256 units. The intermission camera overlooks the
start area.

Two start lights hang from the existing start gantry, centred over the lanes
at `(84, -160, 168)` and `(84, 160, 168)`, facing angle 180 towards the grid.
Each uses the existing Timber Run `models/mapobjects/startlight/startlight.md3`
housing plus a `rally_startlight` entity at the same origin and angle. The
model's hanger rods end at Z=192; four thin metal extensions connect them to
the gantry at Z=256. This lower placement keeps the lenses visible from the
grid in both 4:3 and widescreen views. The housings add no collision, and the
new solid extension rods stay wholly above Z=192. The paired lamps show the
same shared Drag start sequence. The HUD light remains visible as an
additional reference.

## Rebuild

```text
python3 tools/maptools/make_dragstrip.py            # all three maps
python3 tools/maptools/make_dragstrip.py night      # one of: desert, night, airfield
```

The script writes the `.map` files, the arenas, `scripts/q3r_drag.shader`
and the generated textures in `textures/q3r_drag/` (signs, banners, timing
board, rubber, runway concrete, paints, lamps, canvas, windsock). Edit the
script, not the `.map` files. Compile each map with Q3Map2 and the
repository's custom surface flags (repository root as base path):

```text
q3map2 -game quake3 -fs_basegame baseq3r -fs_basepath <repo> -meta -custinfoparms maps/<name>.map
q3map2 -game quake3 -fs_basegame baseq3r -fs_basepath <repo> -vis -saveprt maps/<name>.map
q3map2 -game quake3 -fs_basegame baseq3r -fs_basepath <repo> -light -custinfoparms -fast -super 2 -filter -bounce 1 maps/<name>.map
```

The skies (`textures/q3r_drag/sky_*`) add sky fill light (`q3map_skylight`),
so the light stage takes a while: about 25 minutes for Desert Dragway, 4 for
Airfield Drag, under a minute for Night Drag (8 threads). Ground and buttes
sit in a `func_group` with a coarser lightmap.

## Manual course checks

1. Spawn one driver in each numbered position; check both face the finish.
2. Cross the midpoint and finish through each lane and verify the same gates.
3. Drive across the painted centre and start/finish markings; they should not
   deflect the car or change grip.
4. Confirm the finish gantry does not stop the car and the runoff can be used
   to brake before the end barrier.
5. Repeat the same launch and shift sequence in both lanes and compare times.
6. Check both overhead start lights from the grid. Their reds and green must
   agree with the HUD; a cancelled start must clear them together.

A scripted solo heat (throttle in N, first gear after the countdown, four
upshifts) finished on all three maps: "Player won the Drag heat", ET about
12.8 to 13.0 s on the quarter mile and 8.1 s on the eighth mile.
